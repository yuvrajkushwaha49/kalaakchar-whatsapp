# User Roles & Team Permissions

## Role Matrix
- **SuperAdmin**: Platform master administrator. Manages all tenant businesses, views global analytics, platform Meta App configurations, and AI Support management.
- **Owner**: Full administrative control over their business workspace, billing, number connections, and team member management.
- **Admin**: Can create campaigns, templates, manage contacts, and configure auto-replies. Cannot change billing plans.
- **Marketer**: Can launch campaigns, design templates, and review delivery analytics.
- **Agent**: Dedicated support agent with access to Team Inbox to answer customer chats and update contact notes.
