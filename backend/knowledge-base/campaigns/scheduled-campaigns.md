# Scheduled & Recurring Campaigns

## Overview
Scheduled Campaigns allow businesses to prepare broadcasts in advance for timed product launches, festival greetings, and weekend promotions.

## Plan Availability
- **Free Trial**: Scheduled Campaigns disabled (Instant dispatch only).
- **Starter Growth**: Scheduled broadcasts enabled (up to 15 campaigns/month).
- **Pro Automation**: Advanced scheduling & recurring broadcasts (up to 50 campaigns/month).
- **Enterprise Scale**: Unlimited scheduled broadcasts.

## How to Schedule
1. In **Campaign Builder**, navigate to the **Schedule** step.
2. Select **Schedule for Later**.
3. Choose the target date, time, and timezone (defaults to `Asia/Kolkata`).
4. Click **Schedule Campaign**. The status will be marked as `scheduled`.
5. The platform background scheduler checks pending dispatches every 15 seconds and triggers automatic dispatch when the target timestamp arrives.
