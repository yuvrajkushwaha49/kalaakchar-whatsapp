# Campaign Reports & Analytics

## Real-Time Metrics
Every campaign report displays:
- **Total Recipients**: Total audience targeted.
- **Sent**: Successfully dispatched from server to Meta Graph API.
- **Delivered**: Successfully received on customer handset (double grey tick).
- **Read**: Opened and viewed by recipient (blue ticks).
- **Failed**: Undelivered (due to invalid number, blocked contact, or phone powered off).

## Exporting Reports
- Click **Export CSV** or **Excel** on any completed campaign to download individual recipient delivery timestamps and status logs.
