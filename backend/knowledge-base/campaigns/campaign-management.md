# Campaign Management & Preflight Verification

## Campaign Creation Workflow
1. Navigate to **Campaigns** ➔ Click **Create Campaign**.
2. **Step 1: Campaign Setup**:
   - Provide Campaign Name and Purpose.
   - Select connected WhatsApp Sender Number.
3. **Step 2: Template Selection**:
   - Choose an APPROVED WhatsApp Template (e.g. `jaspers_market_plain_text_v1`).
   - If the template requires dynamic variables (`{{1}}`, `{{2}}`), map them to customer contact fields (e.g. `{{1}}` ➔ Contact Name).
4. **Step 3: Audience Targeting**:
   - Select specific customer tags (e.g. `VIP`, `Festive`) or choose **All Contacts**.
5. **Step 4: Dispatch Mode**:
   - Choose **Send Immediately** or **Schedule for Later**.
6. **Preflight Safety Check**:
   - The platform verifies that the selected template is approved, the sender phone number is active and healthy, and the audience count is within your monthly messaging quota.
