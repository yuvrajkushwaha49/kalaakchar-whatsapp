# Dashboard Analytics & KPI Tracking

## Main KPIs
- **Audience Contacts**: Stored contacts vs. plan quota.
- **Messages Dispatched**: Monthly WhatsApp messages sent vs. allowance.
- **Delivery Rate %**: Percentage of sent messages confirmed delivered by Meta.
- **Read Rate %**: Percentage of delivered messages opened and read by recipients.
- **Active Team Members**: Agent seats used vs. plan limit.
