# WhatsApp Utility & Authentication Templates

## Utility Templates
- Used to confirm user transactions, order dispatch notifications, invoice receipts, shipping tracking, appointment reminders, and account billing alerts.
- Meta charges lower per-conversation fees for Utility templates compared to Marketing templates.

## Authentication Templates
- Used exclusively for sending One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and verification security codes.
- Supports One-Tap Autofill and Copy Code buttons.
- Must not include marketing promotional copy or media headers.
