# WhatsApp Marketing Templates

## Purpose
Marketing templates are used for promotions, product announcements, discount codes, festive sales, and abandoned cart reminders.

## Structure
- **Header (Optional)**: Text, Image, Video, or PDF Document.
- **Body (Required)**: Text up to 1,024 characters with parameters like `{{1}}` for customer name, `{{2}}` for offer value.
- **Footer (Recommended)**: Opt-out text (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe").
- **Buttons (Optional)**: Quick Reply buttons (up to 3) or Call-to-Action buttons (Website URL and Call Phone Number).

## Meta Approval Rules
- Avoid clickbait, misleading claims, or excessive uppercase letters.
- Always provide representative sample values for parameters when submitting for Meta review.
- Approval usually takes between 1 minute to 15 minutes.
