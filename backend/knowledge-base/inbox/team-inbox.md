# Shared Team Inbox & 2-Way Chat

## Live Customer Conversations
- Shared centralized inbox allowing team members to chat with customers in real-time.
- Supports rich text, emojis, image attachments, and document uploads.

## Meta 24-Hour Service Window
- A countdown timer tracks the remaining time in Meta's 24-hour customer service window.
- Within 24 hours, agents can send free-form custom text and media.
- After 24 hours expires, the platform prompts the agent to initiate an approved Message Template to reopen the conversation window.
