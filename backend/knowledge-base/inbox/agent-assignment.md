# Agent Assignment & Internal Collaboration

## Assigning Conversations
- Conversations can be assigned to specific team members or left in the unassigned queue.
- Team members can filter the inbox by **My Chats**, **Unassigned**, or **All Chats**.

## Internal Notes
- Team members can write internal notes directly inside a conversation.
- Notes are highlighted in yellow and are strictly visible to teammates only—the WhatsApp customer never sees internal notes.
