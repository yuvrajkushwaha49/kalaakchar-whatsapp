# Subscription Plans & Pricing Tiers

## Tier Comparison
1. **Free Trial**: ₹0/month
   - 250 Contacts, 500 WhatsApp Messages/mo, 2 Broadcast Campaigns/mo, 1 Team Seat, 1 WhatsApp Number.
   - Community support.
2. **Starter Growth**: ₹2,499/month
   - 2,500 Contacts, 10,000 Messages/mo, 15 Campaigns/mo, 3 Team Seats, 1 WhatsApp Number.
   - Scheduled Broadcasts, AI Copywriter (10/mo), Keyword Auto-Replies (up to 5 rules), Email support.
3. **Pro Automation**: ₹6,499/month
   - 15,000 Contacts, 75,000 Messages/mo, 50 Campaigns/mo, 10 Team Seats, Up to 3 WhatsApp Numbers.
   - Unlimited AI Copywriter (Hindi, Hinglish, English), Keyword Auto-Replies (up to 25 rules), Webhooks & REST API, Priority WhatsApp support.
4. **Enterprise Scale**: ₹19,999/month
   - 100,000+ Contacts, 500,000 Messages/mo, Unlimited Campaigns, 50 Team Seats, Up to 10 WhatsApp Numbers.
   - High-throughput Meta queue (Tier 4), Dedicated SLA & Account Manager.
