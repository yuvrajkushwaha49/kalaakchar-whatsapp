# Payments & Razorpay Integration

## Payment Gateway
- Platform billing is processed securely in Indian Rupees (INR) via **Razorpay**.
- Supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, and Wallets.

## Upgrade Procedure
1. Navigate to **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar.
2. Select your desired plan (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise).
3. Click **Pay with Razorpay**.
4. Complete the checkout popup.
5. Upon payment capture and signature verification, your new plan limits and features are instantly unlocked.
