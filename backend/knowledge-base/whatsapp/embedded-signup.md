# WhatsApp Embedded Signup Guide

## What is Embedded Signup?
Embedded Signup is Meta's streamlined onboarding flow allowing businesses to connect their WhatsApp Business phone number and WABA in under 3 minutes directly through Facebook Login inside our platform.

## Prerequisites
1. A valid Meta Facebook Personal Account that has admin access to your Meta Business Portfolio.
2. A clean phone number that is NOT currently registered on WhatsApp Consumer App or WhatsApp Business Mobile App (must be unregistered or deleted from the mobile app first).
3. Valid business legal name and address for verification.

## Step-by-Step Connection Process
1. Navigate to **WhatsApp Accounts** in the dashboard sidebar.
2. Click the **Connect WhatsApp Account** button.
3. A secure Meta dialog opens: log in to Facebook and select or create your Meta Business Portfolio.
4. Select or enter your WhatsApp Business Profile display name and category.
5. Enter your phone number and verify ownership via SMS or Voice OTP code.
6. Grant permissions (`whatsapp_business_messaging`, `whatsapp_business_management`).
7. Once confirmed, Meta redirects back to our platform callback URL (`/whatsapp/callback`) and saves your connected WABA and Phone Number ID instantly.
