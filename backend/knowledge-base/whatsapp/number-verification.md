# WhatsApp Number Verification & Tier Limits

## Meta Messaging Tiers & Daily Limits
Meta automatically assigns messaging limits to every phone number based on customer feedback and block rate:
- **Tier 50**: 50 unique business-initiated conversations every 24 hours (unverified Meta Business Manager).
- **Tier 1 (1,000)**: 1,000 unique customers per 24 hours once Meta Business Portfolio verification is submitted.
- **Tier 2 (10,000)**: 10,000 unique customers per 24 hours when volume thresholds and high Quality Rating are maintained.
- **Tier 3 (100,000)**: 100,000 unique customers per 24 hours.
- **Tier 4 (Unlimited)**: Unlimited unique customers per 24 hours.

## Quality Rating
- **High (Green)**: Low customer block or report rate. Normal dispatch.
- **Medium (Yellow)**: Moderate customer reports. Review message relevancy.
- **Low (Red)**: High opt-out or spam reports. Daily tier limit will be restricted until quality improves.
